## Coverage reconstruction

- Chunk 4 Target ends mid-sentence at `speciem, vel visionem`. Witness A stops
  at that target boundary. Witness B continues through the entire read-only
  context-after passage, ending with the rendering corresponding to
  `qui credentibus lumen`.
- Chunk 5 Target begins with the dependent fragment `electri esse in medio
  venti, vel spiritus` and ends with `Liber generationis Jesu`. Witness A
  renders the opening fragment but stops before cleanly accounting for the
  final quotation fragment. Witness B starts with the following `Ergo` thought,
  completes `Liber generationis Jesu` from context, and continues through the
  entire read-only context-after passage to `pleniusque in Apocalypsi`.
- Chunk 5 Witness B's preamble and `---` fences occur in the persisted raw
  provider response. They were generated by the model, not added by the cache,
  normalization, or UI layer.

The old prompt said that context was read-only, but opened each section with
`<<<` and never supplied a closing delimiter. Mid-sentence chunk boundaries
made the ambiguity worse. Different model behavior against that same malformed
boundary explains the unequal output coverage; it was not caused by different
targets, output-token truncation, cache selection, parsing, or UI clipping.

## Implemented boundary

The witness prompt now uses closed XML-like context/target sections and
ID-bearing target source units. A witness returns one coherent chunk-level
translation plus a compact JSON coverage receipt with ordered source-unit IDs
and one short exact end-marker per unit. Independent per-unit prose is not
requested. The end-marker form replaced an initially tested start/end form
after live responses duplicated whole paragraphs inside mapping quotes and
exhausted both chunk 5 output budgets.

Separate provider-free validation records verify input identity, immutable raw
integrity, provider completion/headroom, exact schema, absence of material
outside the contract, expected unit IDs, ordered end-markers with the final
marker at the translation boundary, reported omissions, suspicious source
copying, and length signals. Two valid witnesses are required before prosecution. One or zero valid
witnesses fails closed; raw proposals remain available for audit and human
review but are not eligible adjudicator bases.

The historical free-text chunk 4/5 proposals cannot prove source-unit coverage
under the new contract and are therefore ineligible. They were locally
re-finalized as `human_review`; their original model outputs were not edited.

## First structured live run

The first live structured run exposed two additional contract issues without
reaching prosecution:

- Chunk 4 Witness A stopped normally at 1140/1500 tokens but translated the
  read-only context-before and then supplied incomplete/copied mappings. It is
  genuinely invalid. Witness B stopped normally at 1199/1800; its translation
  is target-bounded and complete at the coarse source-unit level. Its only
  initial failure was a repeated final marker (`vision`), which is fully
  disambiguated by its end-of-translation position. The deterministic matcher
  now uses order and the final boundary instead of requiring global uniqueness,
  and cached revalidation marks Witness B valid.
- Chunk 5 Witness A stopped at 1500/1500 and Witness B at 1800/1800. Both JSON
  objects were cut off while emitting mappings that duplicated large portions
  of the translation. The new compact contract retains only one capped
  end-marker per unit, and the target is placed before all read-only context.

The resulting safe cached statuses are `single_valid_witness` for chunk 4 and
`no_valid_witnesses` for chunk 5. A new live witness-only run is required to
test contract v2; no prosecutor or adjudicator should run until both gates pass.
